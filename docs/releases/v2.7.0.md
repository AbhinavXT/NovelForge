Hear who's talking.

With Kokoro or another voice that has several speakers, read-aloud now reads dialogue in the speaker's voice and narration in the narrator's.

Open Character voices in the read-aloud panel to choose voices for the narrator, everyone else, and each character in the book's codex. To start, voices are picked for you by gender and accent.

The speaker is worked out from tags like "said Mei", from the only name in the paragraph, or from turn-taking in a back-and-forth. A quote whose speaker can't be told, such as one tagged only "he said", gets the voice for anyone else.

Audiobook export uses the same cast. A change to the cast applies from the next time you press play.

Installs over 2.6.0 as a normal update.
