Fixes for app lock, e-ink mode, translation and reading stats.

App lock: with it on, the app closed as soon as you opened it. It now asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock and opens. If it's stuck for you now, this update fixes it, and your books and settings are all still there.

E-ink mode: turning it off now puts back the app theme, reader theme, reading mode and page animation you had before. Anything you changed while it was on stays as you set it. If e-ink mode is on when you update, turning it off this once leaves the e-ink look; pick your theme again and it works from then on.

Translation: a chapter's translated title shows in the reader as soon as the chapter is translated, instead of after reopening the book.

Reading stats: a short visit to a long chapter counted every word in it; 30 seconds could add 45,000 words. A session now counts only what could be read in its time. Stats recorded before the update keep their old numbers.

Installs over 2.9.2 as a normal update.
