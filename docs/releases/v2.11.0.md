Your library on all your devices, without an account.

Sync: Settings → Sync takes the address and login of your own WebDAV folder, such as Nextcloud or a NAS. Every device you point at the same folder then keeps the same library, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, categories, per-book looks and reading stats. There is still no account and no NovelForge server, and nothing is sent until you set it up.

- It syncs when the app opens or comes back to the front, two minutes after you stop reading, every 12 hours, and when you tap Sync now.
- Chapter text, covers, book files, your global reader settings and API keys stay on each device. A book from a website shows up on the other device and downloads its chapters there; its position, bookmarks and highlights arrive on the first sync after the chapter list has loaded. An imported book syncs between devices that have the same file.
- If one device suddenly has many fewer books (more than five, and more than half the library), sync pauses and asks before removing them elsewhere. You can keep them instead.
- Restoring a backup doesn't push its older positions and notes to your other devices.
- The address must be https. For a server with a self-signed certificate, Test connection shows its fingerprint and you can choose to trust that one certificate.
- Your password is stored encrypted on the phone and left out of backups. The folder itself is not end-to-end encrypted: whoever runs the server can read the titles, highlights and notes kept there.
- Moving a book to another source removes the old one from your other devices and adds the new one there.
- Disconnect removes the address and password from that device only. Nothing on the server or your other devices is deleted.

Bionic reading: a switch in Quick Settings draws the first part of every word in bold, in scroll and paged modes. Highlights, search and read-aloud are unaffected. Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Thai text is left as it is.

Character voices: giving each character their own voice used to need a multi-speaker voice model. It now works with whatever is reading:

- Google voices: a character can use any voice installed on your phone for the book's language.
- Piper voices: a character can use another Piper voice you've downloaded in the same language. The first line in a new voice takes a moment to load.
- Audio export uses the same cast.

Installs over 2.10.0 as a normal update. The database moves from version 29 to 31.
