Pick up where you left off, and keep what you learn.

Vocabulary: when you look a word up with Define, tap Save word to keep it with its definition, the book and the sentence it came from. Review them as flashcards from the Vocabulary screen in the Library's top bar. Words you know come back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and then 30 days; ones you miss start over.

Previously in...: open a book you last read a week or more ago and a sheet shows the paragraph you stopped on, the characters who mattered most in your last three chapters, and your highlights from them. It never shows anyone you haven't reached yet, and it's built only from what's on your phone.

App lock: Settings → Privacy can ask for your fingerprint, face or screen lock when you open the app and after a minute away. The app is hidden in Recents while it's on.

Incognito mode: reading isn't recorded in history, stats, streaks or your daily goal. Your place in each book still saves.

Book language: hyphenation follows each book's language instead of your phone's. EPUBs bring theirs; for others, pick one under Hyphenate words in Quick Settings.

Also: the medium and large widget sizes show today's minutes against your daily goal, and "Open in codex" from Define opens that character instead of the whole list.

Fixes: dark reader themes no longer show light strips at the top and bottom of the screen. When you move a book to another source, bookmarks in chapters the new source doesn't have move to the nearest chapter; bookmarks moved by 2.3.0 are repaired the same way.

Installs over 2.3.0 as a normal update. Coming from 1.9.0 or older, follow the reinstall steps in the 2.0.0 release notes first.
