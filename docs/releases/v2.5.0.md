Made for e-ink readers.

E-ink mode: Settings → Appearance → E-ink mode. E-ink screens smear every animation and dither every grey, so the mode turns off screen transitions and touch ripples across the app and switches to a pure black-on-white E-ink theme, paged reading and no page animation. You can still change any of those afterwards.

In scroll mode with e-ink on, taps, volume keys, find and read-aloud jump straight to the next spot instead of scrolling there.

Also: the "None" page turn now snaps on taps and volume keys too, not only on swipes.

Not yet covered: auto-scroll still moves smoothly, and books with their own look keep their own theme.

Installs over 2.4.0 as a normal update.
