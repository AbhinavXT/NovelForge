Search Anna's Archive and add books straight to your library. Library → + → Search Anna's Archive. Results are EPUBs; tap one and it's downloaded and imported just like a file you picked yourself, with its cover, chapters and details.

The first search may show Anna's Archive's "I'm not a robot" check. It opens inside the app; complete it and your search carries on. You shouldn't be asked again until the site's cookie expires.

Members of Anna's Archive can enter their secret key (⚙ on the search screen). Signed-in searches skip the check, and downloads use the fast-download servers. Without a key, files come from LibGen, which has the same files. Every download is checked against its MD5 before it's imported, so a wrong file or an error page never reaches your library.

Anna's Archive changes its web address often. If one stops working, NovelForge tries the others it knows and remembers the one that answers. You can also type a working address in the same settings.

The key is kept on your device only: it's left out of Android's cloud backups and device transfers.

Installs over 2.1.0 as a normal update. Coming from 1.9.0 or older, follow the reinstall steps in the 2.0.0 release notes first.
