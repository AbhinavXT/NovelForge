Keep track of what you read, and take your books with you.

Daily goal: set a target of 10 to 90 minutes a day under Reading Stats → Today. A progress bar shows there and on Home.

Reading status: mark each book Reading, Plan to read, On hold, Completed or Dropped from the library's long-press menu, and filter the library by it. Opening a book marks it Reading; reaching the end of a finished book marks it Completed. A status you chose yourself is never changed for you.

Codex in Define: select a character's name and tap Define to see their codex card. Characters you haven't reached yet stay hidden.

Highlights: every highlight from every book on one screen (the quote icon in the Library), filterable by book. Home shows one from your past reading each day. Tap one to jump to it.

This book only: a Quick Settings switch keeps a book's font, size and theme separate from your other books.

Move to another source: long-press a book in the library and pick "Move to another source". You see how many chapters match and where you'll continue before anything changes. Your place, bookmarks, highlights, categories, stats and status come along; downloaded chapters need downloading again from the new source.

Fixes: EPUBs whose chapter headings are separate files keep those chapters in the contents, and scroll mode no longer stops after four chapters.

Installs over 2.2.0 as a normal update. Coming from 1.9.0 or older, follow the reinstall steps in the 2.0.0 release notes first.
