Read raw and machine-translated novels in proper English (or another language).

Pick an engine in Settings → Translation:
- AI, with your key: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible service. Best quality, and it learns names as it goes. Chapter text goes to the provider you pick, who charges you per use.
- On-device: free and private, about 30 MB per language, downloaded once. Rougher on fiction.

Turn translation on for a book with the translate button on its page. Chapters are translated when you open them, or all downloaded chapters at once.

Glossary: each book keeps a list of names, genders and terms so a character is called the same thing in every chapter. The AI adds new names as it meets them, and you can edit any entry.

The translation replaces the chapter text, so search, read-aloud, highlights and export all use it. The original is kept, and you can restore it at any time.

Backups carry glossaries and original chapters. Your API key stays on this phone and is left out of backups.

Installs over 2.5.0 as a normal update.
