Fixes for e-ink mode, translation and paged reading.

E-ink mode: turning it on crashed the app. It now switches over as it should.

Translation: the on-device translator only recognised Chinese, Japanese and Korean, so a French, Spanish, German or other book was left as it was while saying it was translated. It now detects the book's language, offline. If you translated such a book before, open its translation panel, tap Restore original text, then translate it again. Chapter titles are now translated with the text, and Restore original text puts them back too.

Paged mode: the page number no longer sits on top of the last line, and switching full screen on or off keeps you on the paragraph you were reading.

Also: the time-to-finish estimate on a book's page uses the book's real length instead of assuming every chapter is 2,200 words. Reading Stats says "1 day" rather than "1 days", and the "tap to show bars" hint in full screen fades after three seconds instead of covering the first line.

Installs over 2.9.1 as a normal update.
