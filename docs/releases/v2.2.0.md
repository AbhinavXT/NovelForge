Books look like books now, and you can shape how they read.

EPUB formatting: newly imported EPUBs keep their italics, bold, headings, block quotes, pictures and footnotes, in both scroll and paged mode. Tap a footnote to read it without losing your place. Books imported by earlier versions show plain text until they're archived and restored, or imported again.

Contents: the reader has a contents sheet, using the book's own table of contents when it has one.

Book catalogs: browse and search OPDS catalogs from Library → + → "Browse book catalogs", or the "Catalogs" chip on the Search tab. Project Gutenberg is there from the start, and you can add Standard Ebooks or your own Calibre-Web, Kavita or Komga server. Catalogs must use https.

Text replacement: fix a name or a recurring typo while you read, for one book or all of them. Select text and tap Replace, or use Quick Settings. It changes what you see and hear, never the stored text.

Offline dictionaries: import StarDict dictionaries in Settings and look words up with no connection. The online lookup is still there as a fallback.

Typography: import your own TTF or OTF font, turn on hyphenation and set the gap between paragraphs, all in Quick Settings. Paged mode now also honours Justify text and Paragraph indent.

Highlight export: the Markdown export now follows reading order, so chapter 10 no longer lands before chapter 2. Chapters that share a title stay separate, and multi-line notes stay in their quote.

Installs over 2.1.2 as a normal update. Coming from 1.9.0 or older, follow the reinstall steps in the 2.0.0 release notes first.
