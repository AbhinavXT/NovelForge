Downloads that keep going, a library that shows where you are, and a reader with more character.

Downloads: chapter downloads now run in a background queue. Leave the book's page or close the app and they carry on, with a notification that shows progress and has Pause and Cancel. A failed chapter is retried three times. Next to the download button: Next 5 / 10 / 25 unread and All remaining. There's a Download over Wi-Fi only setting, a Queue section on the Downloads screen, and a Downloads row in Settings.

Delete after reading: with Settings → Delete chapters after reading on, moving to the next chapter clears the downloaded text of the chapter before the one you just finished, so one chapter back still reads offline. Chapters with a highlight, a bookmark or a translation are kept, and imported books are never touched.

Library: covers come in Small, Medium and Large. Every book you've opened shows its progress: a bar on the cover in the grid, a bar with "12 / 340" in the list, and a check once finished.

Book page: the Read button, tabs, links and download ticks take their colour from the cover. Grey or blank covers keep the theme colour. Off in e-ink mode, with a switch in Settings → Appearance.

Reader: Quick Settings has a Looks row (Paper, Night, Focus, Compact) that sets theme, font, size, spacing, margins and alignment in one tap, and a Drop cap toggle that enlarges the first letter of each chapter.

Quote cards: the share icon on a highlight in the Highlights screen turns the line into a picture with the book and author, in three looks: Paper, Night and Accent.

Year in review: a card on the stats screen sums up a year, with a page behind it: a bar per month, your three most-read books, days read, longest streak, busiest month and day. It shares as a picture.

AI helpers (optional): if you've set up an AI engine with your own key for translation, two helpers reuse it. "The story so far" summarises the chapters you've read, from the recap sheet or a book's page. "Ask about a character" answers a question on a codex entry from the passages that mention the name. Nothing is sent until you tap, only text up to your reading position is sent, and neither shows without an AI engine configured.

Loading: source lists, the catalog, OPDS lists and the book page show placeholders shaped like the content instead of a spinner. No animation in e-ink mode.

Fixes:
- Range downloads saved nothing for novels not in the library, or for chapters published since the novel was added. They now save.
- A library novel's page opens from the saved chapter list when the source can't be reached.
- A newly added novel no longer shows "N new chapters available" for the whole book.

Installs over 2.9.3 as a normal update. The database moves from version 27 to 29.
