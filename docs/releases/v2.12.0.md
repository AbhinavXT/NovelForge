Audio export gets smaller files and stops being killed in the background.

AAC chapter audio: in the reader's TTS settings, "Audio export format" can now be AAC, which writes `.m4a` chapters (AAC-LC, mono, 64 kbps) about 4 times smaller than WAV. WAV stays the default. The choice is saved in backups, and the audio library lists both formats. The M4B builder accepts `.m4a` and `.wav` chapters.

Faster, and in the background:

- Piper and Kokoro exports use more CPU cores on phones that have several fast ones.- Exports and M4B builds keep running when you leave the app. The notification shows progress and has a Cancel button that stops every running export.

Fixed:

- Building an M4B and merging WAVs couldn't find exported chapters, because they looked in a different folder than the exporter writes to.
- M4B chapter markers were never written; apps now show the chapter boundaries.
- Cancelling an M4B build stops at once, and on Android 9 and older no longer deletes an existing audiobook of the same name.
- Very long sentences are split for system voices.
- An export whose voice model failed to reload now reports an error instead of saving silence.

Known limits:

- Export speed gains depend on the phone; on one with few fast cores there may be none.
- Merge as Audiobook works on WAV exports only. For AAC chapters, build an M4B.
- An M4B needs chapters exported with one voice; chapters at different sample rates fail with a message.

Installs over 2.11.0 as a normal update. No database change.
