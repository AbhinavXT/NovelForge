Anna's Archive, finished.

Downloads now run in the background. Tap "Add to library" and keep searching, add more books, or leave the screen. A notification shows each download's progress, and tapping the finished one opens the book. In the results, every book you've added shows where its download is: waiting, downloading, in your library (tap to open), or failed (tap to retry).

Filters: narrow results by language (English, Spanish, French, German and more) and by type (fiction, non-fiction, comics), or sort by newest, oldest, largest or smallest. Changing a filter re-runs the search.

Editions: tap a result to see the other editions of the same book that turned up in the search, with their size, language and publisher, and pick the one you want.

The Search tab has a new "Anna's Archive" chip next to Browse and All sources. It opens Anna's Archive and searches what you've typed.

Installs over 2.1.1 as a normal update. Coming from 1.9.0 or older, follow the reinstall steps in the 2.0.0 release notes first.
